How it works

Each of the 33 years since 1993 gives one possible future: its flow from today's date onwards, adjusted for how wet the catchment is now. The further ahead, the closer this is to an average year. Tested on every year since 1993/94 with that year left out.

Flow: Environment Agency (Farmoor). Flags: OURCs. Rainfall: ECMWF via Open-Meteo, ERA5. Not an official OURCs product. flagpredictor.com
