How it works

This uses only the river's own flow at Farmoor since 1993, with no weather forecast. A river that has been high or low tends to stay that way for weeks, because the ground and groundwater take time to fill or drain. So we take today's flow and see how each of the last 33 years carried on from a similar starting point. That gives 33 possible futures, and the odds come from counting them. Seasonal rainfall forecasts were tested and didn't improve it, so they're shown for information only. Each past year was forecast from the others to check it works.
